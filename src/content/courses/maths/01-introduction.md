# Course Introduction

Welcome to Mathematics II. This course is designed to build upon your foundational knowledge of algebra and calculus, focusing on linear algebra and multivariable calculus.

## What you will learn

- Linear Transformations
- Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
- Multiple Integrals
- Vector Calculus
