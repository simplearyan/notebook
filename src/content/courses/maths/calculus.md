# Calculus I

Calculus is the mathematical study of continuous change.

## Limits

The concept of a limit is used to describe the behavior of a function as its argument approaches a particular point.

## Derivatives

The derivative of a function represents an infinitesimal change in the function with respect to one of its variables.
