# AQ1.1: Activity Questions 1

Practice questions to test your understanding of vectors.

1. Find the magnitude of vector $\mathbf{v} = (3, 4)$.
2. Calculate the dot product of $(1, 2)$ and $(3, -1)$.
3. Are the vectors $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$ orthogonal?
