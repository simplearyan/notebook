# L1.2: Matrices

A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers arranged in rows and columns.

## Matrix Multiplication

Matrix multiplication is an operation that produces a single matrix from two matrices.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
e & f \\
g & h
\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}
ae+bg & af+bh \\
ce+dg & cf+dh
\end{bmatrix}
$$
