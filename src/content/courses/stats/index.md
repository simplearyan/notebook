# Introduction to Statistics

Welcome to the Statistics course! In this course, we will explore:

- Descriptive Statistics
- Probability Theory
- Inferential Statistics
- Data Visualization

## Getting Started

Statistics is the science of learning from data. It involves collecting, analyzing, interpreting, presenting, and organizing data.
