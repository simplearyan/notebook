# Week 1 Practice Quiz

## Question 1
A fair die is rolled. Let $X$ be the number on the face. What is $E[X]$?

- [ ] 3
- [ ] 3.5
- [ ] 4
- [ ] 2.5

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>
**Answer**: 3.5
**Explanation**: $E[X] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5$
</details>

## Question 2
If $X$ is a discrete random variable, can $P(X=x)$ be negative?

- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>
**Answer**: No
**Explanation**: Probabilities must be non-negative.
</details>
