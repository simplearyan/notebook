# Week 2 Practice Quiz

## Question 1
If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, and $P(X=1) = 0.5$, $P(Y=2) = 0.3$, what is $P(X=1, Y=2)$?

- [ ] 0.8
- [ ] 0.15
- [ ] 0.2
- [ ] 1.5

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>
**Answer**: 0.15
**Explanation**: Since they are independent, $P(X=1, Y=2) = P(X=1) \cdot P(Y=2) = 0.5 \cdot 0.3 = 0.15$.
</details>
